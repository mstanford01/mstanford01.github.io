Music and songwriting

StrumScribe

Write songs, not prompts.

StrumScribe keeps the tools songwriters reach for in one private, offline workspace. Find words, explore harmony, look up playable guitar chords, tune up, keep time, and save lyric, chord, or audio ideas before they disappear.

What it does

A songwriting bench, ready when inspiration hits.

Words that fit

Explore rhymes, near rhymes, syllables, related words, phrases, and other lyric-writing helpers.

Music theory made useful

Work with keys, scales, chords, progressions, the circle of fifths, and practical practice tools.

Guitar across tunings

Browse playable chord shapes, try alternate tunings, identify chords, and save custom tunings.

Tune, time, and capture

Use chromatic and strobe tuners, a metronome, and flexible idea cards with text, chords, and audio.
