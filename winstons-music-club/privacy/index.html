Winston’s Music Club Privacy Policy

Updated September 14, 2026 · Developer: Matthew Stanford

No accounts, advertising, or tracking

Winston’s Music Club does not require an account. It contains no advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking identifiers, or in-app purchases. The app does not send learning activity or microphone audio to the developer.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the selected instrument, settings, lesson and practice progress, achievements, saved compositions, song preferences, and short-plan position locally. Progress is kept separately for each instrument. This information is not uploaded by the app. There is no app-provided cloud synchronization; operating-system device backups may include local app data.

Optional microphone use

With your permission, the tuner, note matcher, clapping activities, and optional iOS 27 sound garden process microphone audio in memory on your device. The app does not create microphone recording files, upload audio, or share it with the developer. You can stop listening, leave the activity, or revoke microphone access in Settings. Other learning activities remain available without microphone permission.

Read-aloud instructions

The app uses Apple’s system speech synthesis to speak its own instructional text. It does not need to record a child’s voice for this feature. Voice availability and device settings are managed by iOS.

Print and share

A grown-up may create a printable guide and choose where to send or save it using the system share sheet. Generated guides contain instrument learning material, not a child’s personal information or progress. The destination chosen by the grown-up may have its own privacy practices.

Support and external websites

External links require a grown-up step. If you choose to email support, the developer receives the email address, message, and any attachments you send, and uses them to respond to your request. Please do not send children’s personal information or voice recordings. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address below, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process normal web-request information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its service. External websites, the App Store, and email services have their own privacy practices.

Children’s privacy

The app is designed for beginner learners and does not ask children for their name, age, email, school, location, or a profile. The grown-up challenge does not verify identity or store its answers.

Deleting local information

Deleting the app removes its local data from that device. Manage any device backups separately in system settings. The developer cannot retrieve or delete local practice data remotely.

Changes and questions

If privacy practices change, this page will be updated. Questions: mstanford01@gmail.com.
