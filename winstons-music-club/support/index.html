Winston’s Music Club Support

Parents and teachers can contact Matthew Stanford at mstanford01@gmail.com.

Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, chosen instrument, and what happened. Please do not send a child’s name, school, voice recordings, or other personal details.

Where should we begin?

Choose an instrument, then use “A little plan for today” or “Not sure where to start?” Assembly and first sounds are best explored with a teacher or grown-up.

Can we use it without a microphone?

Yes. Reading, listening examples, screen tapping, duets, and instrument guides work without microphone access. Microphone permission is requested only for listening activities you choose to start.

Why can’t the app hear a note?

Wait until the example finishes, choose a quiet room, and keep the microphone uncovered. Check the chosen instrument and microphone permission in Settings. The app estimates pitch and may be affected by room noise. A teacher can help when a sound feels difficult.

Does a milestone mean my child mastered a skill?

No. Each message describes an app event or a step the learner says they tried. Completing a duet guide does not mean the app checked their playing.

Where is progress saved?

On the device, separately for each instrument. The app does not offer account or cloud synchronization. System device backups may include app data.

Can we print a guide?

Open My instrument → For parents & teachers, complete the grown-up step, and make the printable practice and note guide. Then use the share sheet to print or save it.

Where are read-aloud instructions?

Look for “Read it to me” in lessons, assembly, music words, short plans, and help. You can stop or replay it. Starting a new audio activity stops the reading voice.

What does it cost?

The app is intended to be free, with no ads or paid lesson gates. Optional links to the developer’s other apps are in the grown-up corner.
