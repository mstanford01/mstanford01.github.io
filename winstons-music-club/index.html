Winston’s Music Club

A welcoming music buddy for new clarinet, alto saxophone, flute, trumpet, and trombone players.

Preparing for release on iPhone and iPad. The app is designed to be free, with no ads, subscriptions, or paid lesson gates.

Learn with Winston

Explore note reading, rhythm and clapping, listening, instrument assembly, and gentle play-along activities. Winston the whippet is learning beside you.

Short practice plans remember your place. Ask Winston for help, hear instructions read aloud, and celebrate small accomplishments at your own pace.

No rankings. No streak pressure. Taking a rest is welcome.

For families and teachers

Designed for beginners, roughly grades 4–7. The app complements a teacher’s instruction; it cannot assess posture, instrument setup, or the quality of an entire performance.

Lessons and sounds are bundled for offline use. Microphone activities are optional and process audio on the device. There are no child accounts.
